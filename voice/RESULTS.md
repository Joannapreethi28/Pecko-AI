- The pre-agreed rule (8-word first phrase ≤ 150 ms **under co-run**) picks **lessac-low** on this ultrabook: medium measured **177 ms** (27 ms over), low 117 ms.
- Reasons it is not final yet: (1) this is an i7-1365U ultrabook, not the judged machine; (2) Voice's first phrase is capped at 6 words by the chunker, and the 3-word medium time is 90 ms; (3) the full cache serves first audio for 30/39 unseen turns with no synthesis at all.
